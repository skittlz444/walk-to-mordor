## 1. Pairs — is the top-2 the right pair?

All four pairs stand. No candidate outside any pair is clearly better.

- **Bronze (a-112358 / a-271828):** correct. The B-variant rejects are genuinely defective (b-161803 missing lower-left dome chunk, b-271828 dome crack, b-141421 keyhole notch, b-112358/b-173205 displaced stones, b-314159 spike-stone stuck-on — all confirmed on the bronze sheet). a-314159's concentric-ring dome does read slightly glossier/CG; a-141421 has a crack across the left dome plus a pit at 10 o'clock. Nothing beats the pair.
- **Silver (a-314159 / a-271828):** correct. Best outside candidate is **a-141421** (clean needle rays, pale oval stone, no visible defect) — it is a legitimately good third, but its bezel is plainer and its tone is darker/less legible than a-314159's lobed bezel; it does not beat A.
- **Gold (a-314159 / b-314159):** correct, but with a record gap — see MUST/should item below. Best outside candidate is **a-112358** (full-gold disc, beaded ring, big dome stone — the most "bar-gold" read in the rank), yet its rim measures as repeated straight lobes (octagonal-family silhouette, same collision the shortlist already used to kill b-112358 against silver a-314159) and its stone dome is polished-glossy, reading as brass rather than chased gold. It does not beat A. No regen needed; just document it.
- **Mithril (b-314159 / b-173205):** correct. b-161803 is the closest runner (dense arm engraving) but darker and busier; a-141421/a-161803 have notched/stepped arm edges that visibly chop the star silhouette; a-314159/b-161803/a-173205 stones show hard facet planes.

## 2. Naturalness

Overall the set reads hand-forged. Ranked strongest → weakest:

- **Strongest: gold a-314159** — dimensional filigree, hand-set beads, pitted iron field; the clearest "made by a smith" surface in the whole set. **Mithril b-314159** is a close second: crisp chasing, laurel sprigs, believable wear on the arm ridges.
- **Bronze a-112358** — irregular planished bezel is convincing; two mild CG tells: the dome's broad specular sweep is a smooth rendered arc (no tool marks break it), and the upper-left bezel cells merge into a muddy dark mass (invisible at stamped size).
- **Weakest element of the picks: silver a-314159's stone** (verified at 512) — polished glass-marble look with a hard-edged white highlight panel and vein streaks. It passes as a moonstone/quartz cabochon, and the bezel/ray work around it is genuinely hand-chased, so it stays acceptable — but it is the single most computer-suggestive region across all four picks. Mithril b-314159's stone has a soft vertical gradient + internal nebula swirl (mildly painted-glass read); plausible as a lab gem, borderline.

## 3. Tier escalation

Good. The silhouette ladder is the strongest thing here: circle (bronze) → octagon (silver) → hex (gold) → four-point star (mithril), so the tiers discriminate at a glance even before material. Ornament steps up bronze→silver→gold without noise: bronze is a bare dome, silver adds engraving + fancy bezel, gold adds a full jeweled cartouche. **Mithril is deliberately restrained rather than more ornate than gold** — premium comes from star form, pale iridescent metal, and the teal gem. That is a defensible "distilled elegance" reading, but it is a real characteristic: mithril reads ~30% less ornament-dense than gold. If the intent is "strictly more ornate per tier," mithril fails; if it is "more refined," it works. My read: it works — gold should be the loud tier, mithril the quiet apex.

## 4. Stamped fit (six frames)

All six seats work. Per-frame specifics from the detail sheet:

- **Journey:** boss drops into the frame's own 6 o'clock dimple; contact shadow reads against the navy well above the braid. Natural.
- **Habit:** covers the band's oval rivet; shadow visible on the brushed steel. Natural. (Mithril caveat: pale star on pale steel is the one low-contrast seat — the teal stone carries the read.)
- **Challenge:** boss seats over the centre scallop of the bottom clasp and reads as a jewel set into the clasp; shadow merges into the black band (fine). Good.
- **Book:** the boss lands exactly between the two gold cord collars and reads as a clamped spine-end ornament — this is a plus, not a clash, but the round bosses' left edges do graze the left collar (visible on Bronze A). The mithril star's long horizontal arms touch **both** collars. Tolerable at final size; if it ever looks pinched, the bosses need a hair more clearance (i.e. ~4–6% scale-down), not a re-stamp.
- **Community:** the scroll/wave ornament behind is partially covered and peeks symmetrically around silver/gold — slightly busier than the other seats but reads as a boss mounted over ornament. Acceptable.
- **Field Guide:** sits over the band dimple onto the grey field; shadow and bezel both read. Clean.

Contact shadow: consistent soft drop, no halo from the BiRefNet cutouts on any of the 48 stamped cells, no visible clipping at the y=1024 canvas edge.

## 5. Pick-by-pick recommendation

- **Bronze: agree — a-112358 (A).** Calmer dome, Irregular scallops read more hand-done than B's rounder, more regular bezel dimples; B also carries a nick in the dome near 12/1 o'clock and swirl-chasing that turns noisy at tier 1.
- **Silver: agree — a-314159 (A).** The lobed bezel is exactly what separates it from B's plain faceted rim; B additionally has the top-bezel hairline crack. A's stone gloss (§2) is the only cost, and it doesn't outweigh the bezel.
- **Gold: agree, with a caveat — a-314159 (A).** At full size A is unambiguously the better object (iron-vs-gold contrast + dimensional filigree). At the stamped ~90 px size, B's bolder composition pushes more contrast — if the final 15 composites show A mushing into a gold blob on the gold Journey/Community bands, b-314159 is the designated fallback. Decision stays A until the frame+boss composites say otherwise.
- **Mithril: agree — b-314159 (A).** Crisper star, better stone dome, laurel sprigs give every quadrant texture; B's round stone is smaller with heavier arms.

## 6. 96/200 px

At the locked 90×90 boss box on the 1024 master: **512 → ≈45 px, 200 thumb → ≈17.6 px, 96 → ≈8.4 px.**

- **200 px:** all eight finalists read as a ranked colour cue — copper+amber, white octagon, gold+dark centre, teal sparkle. Silhouettes survive for silver (octagon edge-on edges) and mithril (star twinkle). Circle/hex collapse to round blobs but colour still separates them. Fine.
- **96 px:** effectively a dot. Only mithril's star retains any silhouette. This is consistent with the standing decision (card label authoritative, boss is the visual cue), so no change — but note gold-on-gold (Journey/Community/Field Guide bands) is the weakest 96 px combination; at that size the dark backing well and the iron hex rim are what keep Gold A separable.

**Mithril dominance:** yes, slightly — it is the only boss whose vertical arms extend past the band top *and* bottom, so on the full-frame sheet it reads as a spike/pin rather than a rivet, and can escape the 90 px tile's visual weight class. I would **not** shrink it: it is the apex rank's intentional emphasis and the best-reading boss at 200 px. If the user finds it aggressive on Habit (pale-on-pale, least grounded seat), scale the mithril placement to ~88–90% as a nicety.

## Findings (must / should / nice)

- **MUST:** none in the art or the stamping.
- **SHOULD — shortlist record gap (gold):** `boss-shortlist.json` line 49 rejects b-112358 for the octagonal-silhouette collision but never accounts for **gold a-112358** at all — it is neither finalist nor listed rejected, despite the same ~8-lobe silhouette issue plus a glossy polished dome. The pair survives on the merits, but the audit trail should record why a-112358 was passed over before the boss decision is locked.
- **SHOULD — silver a-314159 stone:** at 512 the cabochon shows a hard-edged white highlight panel with marble veining — the most CG-suggestive surface among the four recommendees. Acceptable as moonstone; flag it to the user since they know three other silver candidates (a-141421 notably) have softer stones if it bothers them.
- **NICE — Book frame clearance:** round bosses graze the left gold collar; mithril arms touch both collars. Verdict at final size; only act if the composites look pinched.
- **NICE — mithril on Habit:** pale star on brushed steel is the lowest-contrast seat; the teal stone compensates.
- **NICE — bronze a-112358 polish tells:** smooth specular sweep on the dome and mud-merged bezel cells upper-left; both invisible at stamped size, no action.
- **NICE — 96 px tier discrimination:** bosses reduce to colour dots; acceptable per standing decisions, worth remembering when the production 96 px set is cut.
