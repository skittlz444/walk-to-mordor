# Badge Composite Review — Shape, Coherence, and Rank/Frame Stacking

**Date:** 2026-11-02

**Scope:** nine recommended badges, composited at 512 px and 96 px. No application code, no production assets. This report decides the shape and the rank/frame treatment before task 8.6.

> **Catalog update:** the catalog is now **15 achievements** (the nine direction-test badges plus the Glittering Caves return badge, three new habit badges and two community badges). The F1/circle decision applies to all of them; the six new achievements still need their composites.

## Verdict

| Decision | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Shape | **Circle** — both reviewers, independently |
| Rank + frame | **F1: family frame + 6 o'clock rank boss, no rank ring** |
| Coherence | **3/5** — one product, not yet one intentional collection |
| Hexagon | **Park it.** Masked hexagons fail; bespoke hexagons are promising but need a full nine-emblem re-run and a style shift |

The two independent reviews agree on the two structural calls: **circle**, and **stop using a concentric rank ring**. They differ on how strongly to colour the rank: F1 keeps the family metal and uses a small boss; F2 plates the whole frame in the rank alloy. F1 is the stronger set; F2 is the stronger pure rank read.

## Findings

### 1. The rank ring is a third decorative ring, not a rank

At 1016 px the ring band is 40 px; at 96 px it is **3.8 px**. The 3/4/5/6 studs are about 2 px and cannot be counted. Four metals collapse to two visual families — bronze≈gold (warm), silver≈mithril (cool) — and gold-on-gold disappears entirely against the journey frame. In stack A the ring reads as a separate element on only two of nine badges (Khazad-dûm and Home at Sunset); the other seven merge with the emblem rim or the frame. That is the largest single source of the "cobbled together" read: the set claims four ranks but shows two inconsistent ring states.

### 2. Variant E moves the ring but does not solve it

E puts the rank at the outermost edge, which is the right *position*, but:

- gold-on-gold still disappears (Rauros, Mordor);
- the rank band covers the outer 46 px of the 68 px family frame, reducing the journey laurel to ~2 px at 96 px;
- the emblem is scaled to 880 to meet the frame opening, which crops edge-composed art (First Steps, Golden Wood, Mordor, Khazad-dûm);
- the habit pair splits into bronze-rimmed and gold-rimmed silhouettes.

E is the worst of the four for set coherence, even though its intent is right.

### 3. Hexagons: masked fails, bespoke is promising but not this change

The masked hexagon reads as a **coin in a tray**: the circular dome, vignette, and baked rim survive inside the hexagonal frame; the corners are dead space; the art is clipped at the sides. The bespoke hexagon grid is genuinely good — the ferry water fills the bottom point, the door step fills the corners — but it is a **different art style** (painted portal illustration vs enamel coin), needs nine new emblems, a re-shortlist, and hex frame/rank masters. Pointy-top hexagons are also 880 wide × 1016 tall, about 17% less area than the circle in the same card box, and read smaller at 96 px. Both reviewers choose **circle**; the bespoke hex masters are archived as the seed for a future optional family (challenge/community), not a change to this set.

### 4. Set coherence is 3/5 — the frame system holds, the fields do not

Both reviewers score coherence **3/5**. The shared enamel medium, the 1016/880 geometry, and the family frames are real glue. The specific breaks:

- **Four field treatments:** glossy dome (Buckleberry, Golden Wood, Faithful Walker), matte flat (Weathertop, Khazad-dûm, Rauros, Mordor), full-bleed pastel gradient (Home at Sunset), and no field (First Steps). At 96 px the eye sees two lighting models and one postcard.
- **Palette outliers:** Home at Sunset (magenta/pink/orange pastel) and Faithful Walker (very saturated cobalt + bright gold). First Steps is brighter than the dark-fantasy key.
- **Habit vs journey reads as silver vs gold**, i.e. as a metal tier, not a family. The habit frame's copper pinstripe and seven notches — the actual family cue — disappear at 96 px.
- **Motif density ranges from pin to painted plate:** Golden Wood/Rauros are ~60% empty field; First Steps/Khazad-dûm are edge-to-edge.
- **Rank inconsistency** (finding 1) makes some badges look like 2-ring objects and others 3-ring objects for colour reasons alone.

## Recommendation: F1 — circle, family frame + 6 o'clock rank boss

**Stack:** emblem 800 → family frame 880–1016 → 6 o'clock rank boss. **No rank ring.**

The boss is a single cabochon mounted on the frame band, not a concentric element:

- 70 px dark bezel, 54 px rank-metal disc, 25 px gem at 1024, centred at about (511, 979);
- bronze / silver / gold / mithril metal with a dark bezel so it separates from both gold and steel;
- at 96 px it reads as a ~6.6 px coloured dot at 6 o'clock;
- the **rank label on the card remains authoritative**; the boss is a visual cue, not a second data source.

Why F1 over F2:

| | F1 — frame + boss | F2 — plated frame + boss |
|---|---|---|
| Family identity | Preserved: gold laurel vs steel/copper notches | Lost: the whole band becomes the rank alloy |
| Rank read at 96 px | Subtle coloured dot; label does the exact tier | Strong: the whole ring changes colour |
| Emblem/frame regen | None | None, but the family metal is retired |
| Set coherence | Stronger — one frame language per family | Stronger rank axis, weaker family axis |

F2 is the rank-forward option if the product decides the family metal is expendable. It is not the default.

## Coherence fixes

### Must

1. **Adopt F1.** Remove the rank ring from the artwork; keep the family frame; add the 6 o'clock boss. Applies to all nine composites and to `design.md` / `tasks.md` 8.6.
2. **Group by collection in the UI.** The journey seven and habit two should not be presented as a flat nine-grid; the collection sections already planned in the spec do this. The habit pair is a family, not a lower tier.
3. **Fix the two weakest faces with existing finalists.** Swap Home at Sunset to `b-161803` (aged bronze, amber/rust sunset, muted roses) and First Steps to `b-141421` (full door, step and margin, aged metal). Both are already in the shortlist; this is a pick change, not a regeneration.
4. **Normalize frame geometry before 8.6.** The processed frames measure about 993 outer / 872 inner, not the plan's 1016/880; the emblem art is about 776, not 800. Re-normalize the outer to 1016 and re-cut the inner at 880 before the final composite. The direction is valid; the band is about 61 px instead of 68 px.

### Should

5. **Strengthen the habit family cue.** Widen/brighten the copper inner line and notch caps so the habit frame reads as a family at 96 px, not as a silver rank tier. One frame edit; no emblem regen.
6. **Field discipline pass.** One rule: matte flat base + soft top-light gradient, no hard white dome. Knock back the dome on Buckleberry/Golden Wood/Faithful Walker; add the same soft gradient to Weathertop/Khazad-dûm/Rauros/Mordor; flatten Home's pastel field. A grade/LUT pass first; regen only if the dome is baked into the art.
7. **Desaturate Faithful Walker** by ~15% and add the common field gradient. It is the most mobile-game read in the set.
8. **Test the boss at 200 px** (the production thumb) as well as 96 px, and add A/D/F1 to the legibility sheet so the comparison is fair.

### Nice

9. **Archive the bespoke hexagon masters** in `selections.md` as the answer to "what would hexagon art be" — parked, not lost. A future challenge/community family could use the shape.
10. **Give the boss a second axis** (round/square/hex/star cut) if the product wants rank readable without the label. Colour alone cannot carry four tiers at 96 px.
11. **Family mark at 12 o'clock** (leaf/rune for journey, iron/copper mark for habit) to keep the family readable when the laurel/notches blur.
12. **1 px dark keyline** between emblem and frame in the composite template; separates art from metal and makes the emblem sit in the frame.
