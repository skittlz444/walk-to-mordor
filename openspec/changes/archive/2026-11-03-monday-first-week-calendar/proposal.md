# Proposal

## Why

The walk tracker calendar and the stats heatmap render weeks Sunday→Saturday, but the app's weekly achievements are defined on Monday–Sunday weeks: the Faithful Walker badge requires a walk "on every day (Monday through Sunday)" and Hundred-K Week requires 100 km "within a Monday–Sunday calendar week". The UI therefore displays a different week than the one those badges measure, which makes progress hard to read. Switching the calendar surfaces to a Monday-first week aligns the displayed week with the badges and with the ISO week convention.

## What Changes

- Journey page week view is anchored to Monday and its headers read `Mon Tue Wed Thu Fri Sat Sun`.
- Journey page month view headers read `Mon … Sun`, and the first row starts on the Monday on or before the 1st of the month instead of the preceding Sunday.
- The Map page calendar bottom sheet makes the same two changes, so both surfaces stay identical.
- Stats → Heatmap day columns read `Mon … Sun` and rows are grouped Monday→Sunday, with leading padding moved to the front of the first partial week.
- The four duplicated weekday label arrays and week-start calculations in the legacy calendar module are consolidated into shared helpers so the surfaces cannot drift apart again.
- Regression coverage is added for weekday ordering and for a real date landing in the column matching its weekday.

User-visible week grouping changes; no API, database, or stored-data changes. Existing logged walks, totals, and streaks are unaffected.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `calendar-week-display`: the weekday ordering, week start, and weekday-label alignment used by every calendar-style week display (journey calendar week/month views, map calendar sheet, stats heatmap).

### Modified Capabilities

<!-- None: no existing spec covers weekday ordering or calendar grid layout. Achievement specs already describe Monday–Sunday weeks and remain accurate. -->

## Impact

- `public/js/calendar.js` — all four render paths (`renderWeekView`, `renderMonthView`, `renderSheetWeekView`, `renderSheetMonthView`).
- `client/src/components/HeatmapCalendar.tsx` — day labels and week grouping/padding.
- `client/src/components/__tests__/HeatmapCalendar.test.tsx` and `tests/ui/navigation.spec.js` — coverage for the new ordering.
- `public/js/client/islands.js` and the service-worker cache version are regenerated by the normal build; PWA clients pick up the change after the cache version bump.
- No backend, migration, or dependency changes. `src/achievement-awards.ts` already treats weeks as Monday–Sunday and needs no modification.
